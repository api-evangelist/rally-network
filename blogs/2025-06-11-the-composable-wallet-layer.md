What Does ‘Composable’ Mean for Wallets?Composability is the ability to build your wallet stack piece by piece—choosing your own auth, onboarding, and UX. Instead of inheriting a fixed experience from a third-party service, you define it end to end. If you’ve built with other SDKs, you know it’s fast but rigid.
